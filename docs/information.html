Wildfire Safety Information

Learn how to prepare your home, protect your family, and respond safely during a wildfire emergency.

Inside Your Home

Prepare an emergency supply kit and develop a wildfire evacuation plan for your household.

Know your community's emergency plan, evacuation destinations, and escape routes.

When preparing to evacuate, close windows and doors, but leave them unlocked if advised by fire authorities.

Remove flammable curtains and shades, and move flammable furniture away from windows and doors.

Follow local emergency guidance about shutting off gas and pilot lights, turning off air conditioning, and leaving lights on for firefighters.

Outside Your Home

Bring flammable outdoor items inside and move propane grills away from buildings when it is safe to do so.

Turn off propane tanks if directed. Connect garden hoses for firefighters, but do not leave sprinklers or water running unnecessarily.

Leave exterior lights on so firefighters can more easily identify your home.

Keep your vehicle ready for evacuation, with your emergency supplies inside and your keys accessible.

Follow fire department guidance about roof access and protecting vents from flying embers.

Monitor wildfire conditions, follow evacuation orders, and check on neighbors when it is safe to do so.

Animals and Pets

Locate your pets and keep them nearby during wildfire threats.

Prepare carriers, leashes, food, water, and any necessary medications for your animals.

If you own farm animals, prepare transportation arrangements and consider moving them to a safe location before conditions worsen.

Wildfire Evacuation Steps

Review your household evacuation plan and follow instructions from local emergency officials.

Make sure your emergency supplies are accessible and your vehicle is ready to leave.

Protect yourself against heat and flying embers by wearing long sleeves, long pants, sturdy shoes, and appropriate eye protection.

Choose protective clothing made from natural fibers, such as cotton, when possible.

Leave immediately when ordered to evacuate. Do not delay departure to complete home preparation tasks.
