KNOW WHAT TO DO

Protect what matters.

Simple steps can make a difference when preparing for wildfire conditions.

01 ⌂

Inside Your Home

Prepare your household and reduce potential fire hazards before an emergency.

  • Prepare an emergency supply kit.
  • Close windows and doors.
  • Remove flammable curtains and shades.
  • Follow official guidance about utilities.
  • Know your evacuation routes.
02 ♧

Outside Your Home

Prepare outdoor spaces and make sure your vehicle is ready to leave.

  • Bring flammable items indoors.
  • Move propane grills when safe.
  • Keep your vehicle ready.
  • Monitor wildfire conditions.
  • Follow fire department instructions.
03 ♡

Animals and Pets

Include every member of your household, including your animals, in your plan.

  • Keep pets nearby during wildfire threats.
  • Prepare carriers and leashes.
  • Pack food and water.
  • Bring necessary medications.
  • Plan transportation for farm animals.
04 ↗

Evacuation Steps

Understand how to respond when emergency officials issue an order.

  • Follow official evacuation instructions.
  • Keep emergency supplies accessible.
  • Wear sturdy protective clothing.
  • Protect yourself from heat and embers.
  • Leave immediately when ordered.

STAY INFORMED

Know where to turn.

Use trusted emergency resources to monitor wildfire activity, transportation conditions, and local safety information.
